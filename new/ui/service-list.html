Our Services

Charter Air Ambulance Service

Charter air ambulance services provide critical medical transportation for patients who require immediate medical attention or need to travel long distances for specialized treatment. These services typically involve a dedicated medical team, including doctors, nurses, and paramedics, who accompany the patient throughout the journey to ensure their safety and medical care.

Charter air ambulance companies often offer a range of aircraft options, including helicopters and fixed-wing aircraft, depending on the distance to be traveled and the medical needs of the patient.

Commercial Stretcher

A commercial stretcher, commonly known as a medical stretcher or gurney, is a vital piece of equipment used in hospitals, ambulances, and other medical facilities to transport patients who are unable to walk on their own. These stretchers come in various designs and configurations, but they typically consist of a flat, padded surface mounted on a wheeled frame.

Business Class Assistance

Business class assistance typically refers to the enhanced services and amenities provided to passengers traveling in the business class cabin of an airline. These services are designed to offer a more comfortable and convenient travel experience compared to economy class. Business class passengers often have dedicated check-in counters or priority lanes to expedite the check-in process.

Ambulance

Ambulances respond to emergency calls, often dispatched by emergency medical services (EMS) systems or via 911 calls. They are equipped to handle a wide range of medical emergencies, including trauma, cardiac arrest, respiratory distress, and other acute medical conditions.

Ambulances transport patients from the scene of an emergency to healthcare facilities such as hospitals, clinics, or specialized care centers. They are equipped with medical equipment and supplies to stabilize patients and provide basic or advanced life support during transit.

Medical Tourism

Medical tourism refers to the practice of traveling to another country to receive medical treatment or care. This phenomenon has become increasingly popular in recent years due to factors such as rising healthcare costs in some countries, long waiting times for certain procedures.

Medical tourism encompasses a wide range of medical procedures and treatments, including elective surgeries (such as cosmetic surgery, dental procedures, and fertility treatments), specialized surgeries (such as cardiac surgery, orthopedic surgery, and bariatric surgery), cancer treatment, organ transplantation, and wellness and preventive care.
