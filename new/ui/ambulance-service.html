Ambulance Service

Ambulance services are equipped to respond quickly to emergency calls, typically within minutes of receiving the request. Emergency medical technicians (EMTs) and paramedics are trained to assess patients' conditions, provide immediate medical care on-site, and transport them to the nearest appropriate medical facility for further treatment.
