About Us

Ashoka Rescue Air Ambulance

Established in 2004, Ashoka Rescue Air Ambulance Services Pvt Ltd offers worldclass medical transportation, through road and air ambulances in India and across the globe. Our comprehensive services include expert air medical evacuation, commercial medical stretchers, medical transport assistance, and medical tourism patient treatment in India and abroad, ensuring effective and timely delivery through proper medical transport assistance.

We cover almost all major cities in India, including Delhi, Mumbai, Chennai, Kolkata, Hyderabad, Bengaluru, Ludhiana, Pana, Punjab, Goa, Bhopal, Raipur, Srinagar, Jodhpur, Udaipur, Kochi, and Trivandrum. How we assist patients

  • At Ashoka Rescue, we specialize in providing comprehensive assistance to patients both commercially and through charter services, ensuring their comfort, safety, and well-being throughout the entire journey.
  • For patients requiring commercial stretcher services, our process begins with a thorough assessment of the patient's medical needs and requirements. We work closely with the patient's medical team to coordinate the necessary arrangements, including securing medical clearances and permissions from airlines. Once preparations are complete, our experienced medical transport assistants and nurses accompany the patient, ensuring continuous monitoring and care throughout the flight. To facilitate the conversion of commercial airline seats into stretchers, we collaborate closely with the airline staff. This typically involves removing rows of seats to create a dedicated space for the stretcher within the aircraft cabin. Our team oversees this process to ensure that the space meets the patient's specific medical requirements, including accommodating all necessary medical equipment securely. Once the stretcher is in place, our medical team carefully transfers the patient onto it, ensuring that all vital medical equipment remains securely attached. Throughout the flight, our team provides continuous monitoring, care, and support to the patient, working closely with the airline staff to coordinate any additional requirements and ensure the patient's comfort and well-being.
  • In addition to commercial stretcher services, we also offer bespoke charter services tailored to the specific needs of the patient. Our charter services are ideal for patients with complex medical conditions or those requiring urgent transportation. We manage every aspect of the transportation process, from arranging specialized medical aircraft to coordinating ground transportation at both ends of the journey. Our team ensures that the patient receives the highest level of care and attention throughout the entire journey, with access to state-of-the-art medical equipment and a dedicated support team.
  • At Ashoka Rescue, our commitment to excellence extends to every aspect of patient care. Whether through commercial stretcher or charter services, we are dedicated to providing compassionate assistance and support to patients and their families during times of need.
